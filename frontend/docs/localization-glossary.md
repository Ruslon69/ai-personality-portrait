Use sentence case for ordinary English headings; **Tarot, Portrait, Numerology, Zodiac** are discipline/product names. Do not title-case Russian/Ukrainian personal pronouns: address readers as **вы / ви**. Question answers retain **я / I / я** where first person is used.

“Личный разбор / personal reflection / особистий розбір” is permitted in explanatory prose; it must not replace the established English **Portrait** navigation name. “Attitude number” is a domain synonym, not an additional user-facing concept; the product consistently describes first impressions. Existing immutable saved wording is not retroactively edited.

Neither “evidence” nor “confidence” means scientific proof. Portrait evidence describes selected answers. Zodiac/Numerology are optional symbolic traditions. Tarot does not promise events or knowledge of another person's thoughts. Do not translate tentative language into certainty, diagnosis or medical claims.

Art/deck collection names stay as approved proper names. Chinese Zodiac remains disabled; its artwork registry is not a new interpretation product.

Spanish uses neutral international **tú** consistently, with first-person answer options. Avoid **vosotros**, regional slang and switching to **usted**. Use sentence case for headings. **Lectura** names the completed experience; **tirada** names its arrangement. **Al derecho / invertida** distinguish orientation from **reverso**, the back of a face-down card. Tarot suit names: **Bastos, Copas, Espadas, Oros**; court ranks: **Sota, Caballero, Reina, Rey**. The number of first impression remains the existing calculation, not a new formula.
